# Safa Life 🌙

**Safa Life** is a modern Islamic lifestyle application designed to help Muslims live a life guided by the Qur'an and Sunnah.  
With a clean design and meaningful features, Safa Life brings everything you need for a spiritually balanced daily life:

- 📖 **Digital Qur'an**: Read and listen to the Qur'an with tafsir.
- 📜 **Hadith & Sunnah**: Access authentic hadiths and prophetic guidance.
- 🕌 **Prayer Times**: Accurate prayer schedule and reminders based on your location.
- 🎥 **Muslim Reels**: Short inspirational videos and Islamic content.
- 📅 **Islamic Events**: Stay updated with lectures, sermons, and community activities.
- 🌱 **Hijrah Program**: Guidance, motivation, and community support for your spiritual journey.
- 💝 **Charity Features (Coming Soon)**: Pay zakat, infaq, sadaqah (including *dawn alms*) and support community causes directly through the app.

✨ With Safa Life, embrace a pure and peaceful life aligned with Islamic values.  
🚀 More features are on the way to make Safa Life your all-in-one Islamic lifestyle companion.
